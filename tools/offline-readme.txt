SUDOKU (OFFLINE)
Just you and a Sudoku, in your web browser. No internet, no Twitch, no OBS.

HOW TO START
  1. Unzip this folder somewhere it can stay, e.g. Documents\Sudoku.
  2. Double-click "Play Sudoku.html". It opens in your browser.

HOW TO PLAY
  Click or tap a cell, then a number (on your keyboard or the number pad).
  Wrong numbers bounce off and count as a mistake.

  Keys:  1-9 place a number      arrows move around
         N   notes on/off        Shift + number  pencils a note
         Backspace clears notes  H  hint (the selected cell)
         P   pause               Enter  next puzzle once solved

  Pick the level (1 Beginner to 20 Nightmare, or Adaptive, which follows
  how you did) and the puzzle type under "Next puzzle".

Your puzzle, best times and settings are saved in this browser, so you can
close it and carry on later.
